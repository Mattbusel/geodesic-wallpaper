A desktop you'll stare at. Officially, that's math.

Glowing trails flow behind your desktop icons, each one the shortest path across a curved surface. It looks like a screensaver. It is actually the geodesic equation, solved live with Christoffel symbols. So the ten minutes you just lost watching it? Technically, you were studying differential geometry.
